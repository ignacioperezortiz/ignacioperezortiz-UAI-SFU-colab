**Method.** Electrical distance of each prosumer = sum of the series self-impedance |Rs + jXs| of the cables from the LV
side of its transformer to its bus (per unit on the model base; 1 p.u. = 0.16 ohm). Each prosumer gets its
percentile p inside its own transformer. In every transformer, round(n/3) prosumers are drawn for the far aggregator
**Agg3** without replacement, with probability proportional to exp(-0.5((p - 0.9)/0.2)^2); the rest
are shuffled and split evenly between Agg1 and Agg2. Seed 20261022.
